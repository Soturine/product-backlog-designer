# Example reports (synthetic)

Fixture reproductions built by `python tools/build_examples.py` from the test-suite payloads of a
fictional community-garden domain (plus a fictional neighbourhood library and a two-developer team
plan for the readiness and planning views). They are not agent-led executions and carry no
real data.

- [generation](generate/report.html)
- [audit of an existing backlog](audit/report.html)
- [comparison of two backlogs](compare/report.html)
- [generation with a blocking question and development readiness](readiness/report.html)
- [team sprint plan: owners, assignees, load by person](plan/plan-report.html)

Open a `report.html` with a double click: it works offline from `file://`.
